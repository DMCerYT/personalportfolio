# Completing the design case studies

Case-study text and artifact slots live in each project’s `src/data/projects/<slug>.js` file. See [the authoring guide](project-authoring.md) for adding and editing entries. Current image slots deliberately show a labeled placeholder rather than implying the illustration is real project work.

To replace a slot, save an image in `public/images/projects/` and add `src: '/images/projects/your-image.webp'` and descriptive `alt` text to that artifact object. Rewrite its caption to explain what the artifact demonstrates and why the decision mattered. Keep exports readable on mobile and remove participant-identifying information.

Prioritize Money Mentor: an affinity-map excerpt, a key prototype flow, and a before/after comparison tied to a specific testing observation. Then add Focus Farmer's embedded interface and reward states, and Atlanta Taste Trek's discovery screens and Figma-to-code comparison.

Before submitting applications, confirm your individual contribution versus the team's work, add dates and team context, and replace the provisional narrative with concrete research findings and design tradeoffs where available. The existing research counts were retained from the original portfolio; no usability improvements or findings were invented. Proposed evaluations are labeled as next steps.

The project listing excludes entries marked `isUnderConstruction`. Money Mentor leads the curated listing; the home page shows the three featured studies. Keep a new project out of the showcase until its case study is ready.
