# Money Mentor narrative and evidence

The published copy lives in `src/data/projects/money-mentor.js`. The narrative follows a specific thread: understand informal budgeting habits, design the budgeting flow, learn where the interface fails to communicate, and revise the first-use experience with the team.

[Summer Abramson’s Atlanta Taste Trek case study](https://summera.framer.ai/cases/att) informed the conversational voice, explicit collaboration, and focus on a concrete design turning point. [QuestMates](https://summera.framer.ai/cases/questmates) informed the connection between research, prototype changes, and reflection. The wording and Money Mentor findings are original to this case study.

## Evidence boundaries

- Milestone 2, p. 1 reports **eight interviews and 26 survey responses**. These replace the unsupported 10+ interviews and 100+ surveys in the earlier portfolio copy.
- Milestone 2, pp. 2–4 reports informal budgets, food spending, and difficulty tracking expenses or saving. The page uses the report’s 54% informal-budget figure and five-of-eight interview finding. The figures are descriptive findings from the team’s sample, not population estimates or causal results.
- Milestone 3 documents ten concepts. Avoid implying that Emmanuel individually created all ten or led the task analysis.
- Milestone 4, pp. 2–3 reports **four Georgia Tech participants**, ages 18–24. Its study script describes task groups with several actions; the page avoids the previous unsupported “five tasks with five participants” claim.
- Milestone 5, p. 9 documents changes following think-aloud and heuristic feedback. It does not document a repeat study or measured improvement. Treat those changes as implemented prototype revisions, not validated outcomes.
- The required-budget onboarding tradeoff is a portfolio interpretation of the final interaction, clearly presented as a future test. The milestones do not show that students preferred optional onboarding.
- Milestone 2’s GenAI disclosure says it guided survey-response development; the exact scope is unclear. Keep it in mind if discussing the study’s methodology in an interview. It does not support claims about synthetic participants or explain the survey counts.
- Milestone 4 discloses AI use for the logo. Do not claim original logo illustration as Emmanuel’s work.
- Do not add project dates, improved financial outcomes, task-success rates, or specific individual concept ownership without further evidence.
